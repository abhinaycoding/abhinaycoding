# 💫 About Me

Somewhere between debugging a smart contract past midnight and shipping a SaaS feature before an 8 AM lecture, I realized I actually love this chaos. I'm a B.Tech CSE student at Ramdeobaba University (Class of 2028) who treats every hackathon like a plot twist waiting to happen — three wins in, and counting.

I build at the intersection of **full-stack apps**, **AI**, and **Web3** — legal-assistance tools powered by Gemini one week, a developer dashboard running on the Stellar testnet the next. As a Blockchain RBU member, I'm equally at home shipping a billing SaaS for MSMEs and poking at chain infra just to see how it ticks.

When I'm not pushing commits, I'm probably reverse-engineering how something works or convincing my terminal to do one more thing it wasn't built for.

> 🚀 **Right now:** building in public, breaking things on purpose, and leveling up in system design + cloud.
